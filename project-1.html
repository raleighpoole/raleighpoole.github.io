Strategic Revenue Optimization &
Automated Pricing Architecture

Case Study: Building an automated Salesforce and Excel ecosystem to execute a risk-mitigated pricing strategy post-acquisition, capturing $200K+ in ARR with near-zero churn.

Executive Summary

Following a corporate acquisition by private investors, optimizing legacy revenue streams became a critical operational priority. Partnering with the Director of Operations, I engineered an end-to-end pricing and risk model targeting long-tenured, deeply discounted accounts. By building a daily-automated data pipeline between Salesforce and a multi-tiered Excel model, we identified and executed targeted rate adjustments. This automated ecosystem captured nearly $17,000 in monthly recurring revenue (MRR)—a 1.5% boost to total company ARR—while maintaining a world-class customer retention rate (>98%).

Phase 1: Salesforce Extraction & Cross-Filtering

A primary challenge was isolating true targets without disrupting add-on products or clients paying full price. Using native Salesforce reporting tools, I developed a refined extraction strategy:

  • Cross-Filtered Account Querying: I generated primary Salesforce Account Reports cross-filtered against Accounts with Subscription Reports. This stripped away accounts that only utilized add-on services (such as digital marketing or standalone tools).
  • Core Product Isolation: By filtering out noise, the query explicitly isolated legacy accounts holding our core platform subscription at a discounted rate for 18+ months, ensuring rate adjustments were applied strictly to primary subscription tiers.

Phase 2: Bounded Modeling & Multi-Criteria Excel Mechanics

To eliminate manual lookup errors, I engineered a dynamic pricing matrix. The model evaluated two independent data dimensions simultaneously—commercial vehicles on-lot alongside active package tier types—to map accounts to their designated rate increase:

Cohort Tier Dealer Inventory Volume Target Increase (%) Model Guardrail Rule
Tier 1 Small Independent (Low On-Lot Volume) + 8% Increase Bounded to Min of % Lift vs. Standard List Price
Tier 2 Mid-Sized Operations + 12% Increase Bounded to Min of % Lift vs. Standard List Price
Tier 3 Large Commercial Hubs + 18% Increase Bounded to Min of % Lift vs. Standard List Price
Tier 4 High-Volume Mega Groups + 24% Increase Bounded to Min of % Lift vs. Standard List Price

Using multi-criteria array formulas, combining boolean logic within XLOOKUP structures, the sheet automatically matched live inventory data against package tiers to reference the exact target adjustment percentage. To protect client relationships, a strict logical guardrail ensured the new rate would never exceed standard list price:

$$ \text{Adjusted Rate} = \min\Big(\text{Current Discounted Rate} \times (1 + R_{\text{tier}}), \, \text{Standard List Price}\Big) $$

Phase 3: Automated Syncing & Recurring Opportunity Pipeline

Instead of creating a static one-time spreadsheet, I architected a repeatable, dynamic analytics ecosystem built for ongoing operational use:

  • Automated Salesforce Integration: Configured automated daily report extractions out of Salesforce, ensuring the master modeling environment automatically refreshed with live account changes, inventory updates, and subscription statuses.
  • Quarterly Opportunity Identification Engine: The underlying nested XLOOKUP architecture allowed leadership to revisit the ecosystem every quarter. As accounts crossed the 18-month tenure threshold or shifted inventory tiers, the system automatically highlighted new low-hanging revenue opportunities.

Phase 4: Tech-Touch Rollout & Financial Results

I partnered directly with Customer Success Managers (CSMs) to translate data outputs into client-facing actions. We configured automated Outreach sequences that gave dealers a generous 3-to-6 month advance notice, easing the transition and explaining the expanded platform value.

  • $200K+ Annualized Revenue Captured: Against an initial projection of $18,000 MRR, the automated model successfully secured $16,900+ in net new MRR ($202,000+ ARR) on the initial pass.
  • Near-Zero Churn (<2% Attrition): Out of ~120 targeted legacy accounts, only 2 dealers canceled citing the price adjustment, proving the high precision of the cohort segmentation.
  • Repeatable RevOps Framework: Provided executive leadership with a permanent, automated tool to monitor pricing parity on a quarterly basis without manual overhead.
